Finish the month. Meet the scientist.

Albania Now connects Albanian youth directly with working professors and researchers. Each sprint is four weeks of free, optional material on one topic — and every student who completes the work joins a live closing lecture by the sprint's scientist, with the room's questions answered out loud.

All material and lectures are delivered in English

Join — it costs nothing

Five minutes, right now: this week's Trace Race — one short Python program, same puzzle for all of Albania. Can you read what it prints? New every Monday. Share your grid.

How it works

One topic. Four weeks. One lecture.

  1. Pick a sprint. Each is one topic, outside your normal coursework, completely optional. Your school just points you here.
  2. Do one lesson a week. A lesson takes two to four hours: a video lecture, an audio brief, reading you can play with, a Colab notebook you run yourself, optional go-deeper material, a quick check, and one thing you build and turn in.
  3. Turn in your four builds. Keep your screenshots and notebooks as you go; the join page explains where they land. A full app — sign-in, automatic progress, certificates — is in the works; nothing is graded by your teachers.
  4. Come to the lecture. Everyone who completes the sprint joins a live video lecture with the sprint's scientist, and can ask them anything. Each lecture is recorded and the recording is posted afterward, so the whole community keeps it.
Between lessons, you are not alone. Each sprint has a Discord channel — students, teachers, and mentors in one room, questions answered between lessons. And milestones are marked in the physical world: finish a sprint and Albania Now mails you the sticker for it. Collect the set.
Sprints are independent. Do one, do all seven, do them in any order. A focused student finishes a sprint in a month.

The sprints

SPRINT 01 · 4 WEEKS

Data Science, from zero

No experience needed

Run real Python in your browser, work real tables, make charts that tell the truth, and finish with an analysis of your own. Closing lecture: Dr. John Santerre, machine-learning faculty, UC Berkeley.

 SPRINT 02 · 4 WEEKS

Planetary Exploration

No experience needed

How we read distant worlds from their light, how spacecraft explore, the moon with weather — and finding a planet in real-style data yourself. Closing lecture: Dr. Conor Nixon, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center.

 SPRINT 03 · 4 WEEKS

Intro to Large Language Models

No code until week 4

What is actually inside the AI everyone talks about — the next-word machine, tokens, training, why it hallucinates — and in week 4 you build a tiny one yourself. Closing lecture: Dr. John Santerre.

 SPRINT 04 · 4 WEEKS

AI Image Analysis on Other Planets

Builds on basic Python

Build a crater detector, teach a machine to sort what spacecraft see, and learn the honest way to report what it found. Closing lecture: Dr. Conor Nixon, NASA — machine learning on planetary images is his real research.

 SPRINT 05 · 4 WEEKS

Leveraging AI in Your Own Education

No code — for every student

Use the AI in your pocket to get measurably better at school — study technique, honest writing, one real documented week. Closing lecture: Dr. Nedelina Teneva.

 SPRINT 06 · 4 WEEKS

Learning to Read Python

Builds on basic Python

Programmers read more than they write — and judging code you didn't write is the AI-era skill. Tracing, shapes, other people's code, and your first real code review. Closing lecture: to be announced.

 SPRINT 07 · 4 WEEKS

Learning to Learn from External Resources

No code — for every student

The map of where answers live, judging quality in a minute, asking questions strangers enjoy answering, and a self-study system you actually run. Works for any subject. Closing lecture: to be announced.

For schools

Albania Now is free. It runs on plain web pages and Google Colab's free tier — no licenses, no student accounts on this site, nothing to install. Schools are distribution partners, not administrators: tell students it exists, lend computer-lab time to phone-only students, and host the sticker ceremony — no grading, no accounts to manage. The material comes from Chicago First, a ten-course AI and programming curriculum built for Chicago high-school students, extended and adapted for Albania by Free Focus, a nonprofit that provides technology services to nonprofits.
