One topic. Four weeks. One lecture.

1 · Pick a sprint

Free, optional, outside coursework. A school just points students at the site — no accounts, no licenses, nothing to install.

2 · One lesson a week

Two to four hours: video lectures, an audio brief, interactive reading, a Colab notebook, optional go-deeper material, a check, and a build turned in to a teacher.

3 · Four builds in

A teacher at the school confirms the turn-ins. That is the whole infrastructure.

4 · The lecture

Everyone who completed joins a live video lecture with the sprint's scientist — and can ask them anything. Recorded and posted afterward.

Between lessons: each sprint has a Discord channel — students, teachers, and mentors in one room.

Milestones are physical: finish a sprint, and Albania Now mails the sticker for it. Collect the set.

The lecture is the point. The material builds the skill; the lecture connects the student to a person who uses it for a living.

What it runs on, and what we ask

Free stack. Plain web pages plus Google Colab's free tier. No platform, no licenses, no student accounts on the site. Student tracking comes later, deliberately — the pilot stays light.

English-first. Albanian students meet English early; the welcome is bilingual, the material is English. Translation follows what the pilot proves.

Honest state. The material is complete and new — the pilot classroom is part of the plan, and what it breaks gets fixed before scale.

A school provides: one teacher willing to collect weekly turn-ins and confirm completions, students, and the school's decision on Google accounts.

Free Focus provides: the site and updates, the sprints, the speakers and lectures, the Discord, the stickers, and onboarding for each school.

The path: pilot schools → grow the catalog and the speaker roster → translate what earns it → open to every school in Albania.
