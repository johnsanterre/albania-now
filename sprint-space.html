The closing lecture

A live hour with Dr. Conor Nixon, NASA

Complete all four builds and you're on the list. The sprint closes with a live video lecture from NASA Goddard Space Flight Center — what it's like to explore Titan for a living, what the next missions will do, and open questions from the room. Your teacher confirms completions and shares the join link. The lecture is recorded and posted afterward, and finishing the sprint earns you its sticker, by mail.

Dr. Conor Nixon
Associate Laboratory Chief, Planetary Systems Laboratory, NASA Goddard Space Flight Center

Deputy Principal Investigator on the Cassini Composite Infrared Spectrometer, and a leading scientist on Titan, Saturn's largest moon — the only moon in the solar system with a substantial atmosphere. He studies the atmospheres of the outer planets with the James Webb Space Telescope, holds degrees from Cambridge, Manchester, and Oxford (DPhil on remote sensing of Titan's atmosphere), and has published more than 200 scientific papers. Week 3 of this sprint is his world.

What you need: a browser, a Google account for Colab (ask your teacher if your school provides one), and English. No installs, no fees. The math never goes past multiplication and square roots.
