The Founding Season

Albania Now runs in seasons. Every sprint's cohort moves on the same calendar, every lecture lands in the same finale week — and the whole community peaks together. This is the first one, and firsts only happen once.

The rhythm, every week

  1. MMonday — the new Trace Race, Crater Hunter terrain, and Find the Lie charts drop. Same puzzles for all of Albania.
  2. WWednesday — the community moment: mentors in the Discord, questions answered.
  3. FFriday — the recap: the week's best share grids, and next week's tease.

The season's shape

Four weeks of sprint lessons, one lesson a week — then the finale week, when every sprint's live lecture happens within a few days: the scientists, the earned seats, the questions. Completers of the Founding Season get the founding sticker (this design is never printed again) and certificates numbered from #1.

Dates

Announced with the first cohort

The Founding Season's start and finale dates are set together with the pilot schools, and will be posted here and on the join page. The weekly games are already live — the season starts before the season starts.

Missed a season? Nothing is lost. Progress carries — pick up in the next season exactly where you stopped, and the lecture seat waits for the four builds, whenever they're done.
