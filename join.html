Mirë se vini · How to join

Start today. It costs nothing.

Albania Now is free and optional, and the material is open — you can begin a sprint this minute, no account, no permission. Joining properly gets you the rest: the community, the lecture list, and the sticker.

Students

Three steps

1. Pick a sprint from the catalog and start week 1 right now — everything works without signing up.
2. Email john.santerre.ai@gmail.com with the subject JOIN — your first name, your school, and which sprint. You'll get the community invite and the current cohort's lecture date back.
3. Do one lesson a week and keep your builds (screenshots and notebooks). Complete all four, and you're on the lecture list — the live video call with the sprint's scientist, plus the sticker, by mail to your school.

Teachers and school directors
Distribution partners — not graders

Albania Now asks nothing of your staff: no grading, no administration, no accounts. What helps: tell your students it exists, give phone-only students a computer-lab slot for the coding sprints, and host the sticker hand-out when your completers earn them. Email john.santerre.ai@gmail.com with subject SCHOOL and we'll send the one-page introduction — in Albanian — you can share with parents and colleagues.

Scientists, engineers, researchers
Give one hour, close one sprint

A sprint's closing lecture is one hour on a video call with students who earned the seat — completed work, real questions. If your field could be a four-week sprint, the material machinery already exists. Email john.santerre.ai@gmail.com with subject SPEAK.

Coming next: a full Albania Now app — sign in, automatic progress tracking, certificates with verification links, cohort calendars. Until then, email is the front door, and everything above is real today.

Privacy, plainly: joining shares your first name, school, and email with Free Focus and nothing else. We never publish student names, never mail to home addresses (stickers go to schools), and delete your information on request.
