The closing lecture

A live hour with Dr. John Santerre

Complete all four builds and you're on the list. The sprint closes with a live video lecture — what large language models can and cannot do, where the field is going, and open questions from the room. Completion is tracked through Albania Now — the join page explains — and completers get the join link directly. The lecture is recorded and posted afterward, and finishing the sprint earns you its sticker, by mail.

Dr. John Santerre
Machine-learning faculty, UC Berkeley and SMU · PhD in computer science, University of Chicago

Teaches machine learning to graduate students at Berkeley and SMU, and was a photojournalist before academia. This sprint distills the opening of his AI curriculum — the mental model everything else stands on.

What you need: a browser and English. Weeks 1–3 need no code at all; week 4 uses Google Colab (a Google account — ask your teacher).
