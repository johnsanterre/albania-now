The closing lecture

A live hour with Dr. John Santerre

Complete all four builds and you're on the list. The sprint closes with a live video lecture — how data science is actually practiced, what it looks like as a career, and open questions from the room. Your teacher confirms completions and shares the join link. The lecture is recorded and posted afterward, and finishing the sprint earns you its sticker, by mail.

Dr. John Santerre
Machine-learning faculty, UC Berkeley and SMU · PhD in computer science, University of Chicago

Teaches machine learning to graduate students at Berkeley and SMU. Before academia he worked as a photojournalist — the habit of looking hard at evidence carried over. This sprint is his graduate material, rebuilt from zero for high-school students.

What you need: a browser, a Google account for Colab (ask your teacher if your school provides one), and English. No installs, no fees.
